BUDGET DESK 1.3.0 — WINDOWS

Updating? First export a JSON backup in the old app under Settings & backup,
then close it. Extract the entire new ZIP to a new folder.
Open BudgetDesk.exe in the BudgetDesk folder. Keep its .config file beside it.

Manuals/English.pdf or .html       English user manual
Manuals/Cesky.pdf or .html         Česká uživatelská příručka
Manuals/Ellinika.pdf or .html      Ελληνικό εγχειρίδιο χρήσης

Support/Test-Sandbox.cmd   Separate, initially empty budget for testing
Support/Try-Demo.cmd       Separate budget with sample data
Support/Start-BudgetDesk.cmd  Starts the app with diagnostics if needed

Choose Item color when creating or editing an account, payment, entry or goal.
The illustrated manuals include the palette and examples for all five tabs.
Open Settings & backup to select the interface language and Light or Dark.
New automatic one-off expenses post at the scheduled time. Existing plans
keep their manual setting until you edit them.
Support/IDEAS.md lists ideas for future versions.

In the source checkout the commands and supporting documents are at the root.
The Windows ZIP keeps them together in Support.
