# Budget Desk 1.1.0

Download **BudgetDesk_v1.1.0_Windows.zip**, extract it into a new folder and open **BudgetDesk.exe**. Export a JSON backup from your previous version before updating. Existing budgets open from the same Windows data folder.

- Choose the receipt currency beside the amount on an expense or income. It defaults to the selected account. For a foreign purchase, confirm the rate and see the exact charge in account currency. Splits, templates, plans, recurring payments, reports and CSV exports carry the corresponding amounts.
- Record local times on daily entries, plans and recurring payments. **Record paid** uses the confirmation time unless you enter an earlier payment time. The original due time remains available. Automatic payments wait until due and catch up once per missed occurrence after restart.
- Names precede their icons. Account, category and icon pickers are alphabetical and let you jump to the first match by typing its initial. Account cards reserve separate areas for the icon, name and balance. Their personal display order is retained.
- Click a column heading for ascending order, click again for descending, and click a third time to restore the original order. Sorting covers every page. Dates and amounts sort by their actual values.
- Open **Manuals/English.html**, **Manuals/Cesky.html** or **Manuals/Ellinika.html** for a complete offline guide. The launch folder contains just the executable, configuration and the **Manuals** and **Support** folders. The demo, test sandbox, source and Windows test results are inside **Support**.
- Version 1.1.0 uses budget schema 5. Earlier stored dates and financial values are retained. When the old budget did not record a time, the time stays blank.
