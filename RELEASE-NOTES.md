# Budget Desk 1.1.1

Under **Accounts & bills**, pressing **Pause / resume** on an active recurring payment turns its entire card red. The white text and bold **Paused** label stay visible when you select another payment or reopen the app. Press **Pause / resume** again to restore the active colors.

Download **BudgetDesk_v1.1.1_Windows.zip**, extract the whole ZIP into a new folder and open **BudgetDesk.exe**. Existing budgets and paused schedules load from the same data folder. **Support/Test-Sandbox.cmd** also reopens your existing 1.1 test budget. The three offline manuals are in **Manuals**.
