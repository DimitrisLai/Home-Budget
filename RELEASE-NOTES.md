# Budget Desk 1.4.0

Download **BudgetDesk_v1.4.0_Windows.zip**, extract it and open **BudgetDesk.exe**. Export a JSON backup from your previous version before updating.

- **Term deposits** is next to **Accounts & bills**. Set an opening date, maturity, currency and optional item color. Choose a regular account to fund the deposit, or enter an existing deposit balance.
- Record transfers into a deposit and partial or full returns, including an actual early withdrawal. Maturity changes the status. Manually record the return when the funds reach your account.
- The first tab and the new tab show the combined balance. Transferring 4,000 CZK from a 10,000 CZK account leaves 6,000 CZK in the account, 4,000 CZK in the deposit and 10,000 CZK overall. Principal transfers appear in history and CSV and are excluded from income and spending.
- The three offline PDF and HTML manuals include a worked example. Existing budgets open with their records intact and an empty deposit list. The test checklist, future ideas and Windows verification results are in **Support**.
