# Budget Desk 1.3.0

Download **BudgetDesk_v1.3.0_Windows.zip**, extract the entire archive to a new folder and open **BudgetDesk.exe**. Export a JSON backup from the previous version before updating.

- **Colors for individual items.** While creating or editing an account, recurring payment, daily transaction, future expense, goal or template, open **Item color**. Choose from 32 named swatches and select **Save**. The selection appears on that card or row in either theme. Choose **Default color** to clear it.
- **Payment history.** A paused recurring payment stays red. Resuming shows its chosen color again. Posted payments copy the color of their schedule, and template drafts start with their template’s color. Old entries keep their own colors after later edits to the source.
- **Illustrated offline guides.** English, Czech and Greek each have a 13-page PDF and a matching HTML guide. The manuals include linked contents, screenshots, the complete palette and detailed examples for accounts, split receipts, exchange rates, scheduled payments, goals, templates, reports and recovery.

Existing budgets open in their usual data folders. The support tools, test checklist, future ideas and Windows verification results are included in **Support**.
